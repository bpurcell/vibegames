About the data & this experiment

Inspired by cambecc/earth. Wind is its bundled historical NOAA GFS sample: 31 Jan 2014, 03:00 UTC, at 10 m above ground, on a 1° grid. This is not live weather.

Particles follow horizontal wind vectors and are visually draped over terrain. They do not model vertical air motion or resolve local mountain winds. Animation time is accelerated.

Elevation uses Mapzen / AWS Terrain Tiles, sampled to a 161 × 161 mesh. Ocean elevations are shown at sea level. Polar regions beyond 84° are excluded.

Terrain sources: Mapzen attribution. Globe imagery: NASA Blue Marble via WebGL Earth. Renderer: Three.js (MIT).
