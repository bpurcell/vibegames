10 mm tile overlap

Print at 100% / actual size, with headers and footers off. Check the 50 mm calibration line before cutting.

A few notes for the workbench
  1. Print, trim the tile margins, and match the overlap marks. Tape the wall pages together.
  2. Roll a slab to your chosen wet thickness. Cut one wall and one floor.
  3. Join the wall edges with a butt seam: no overlap allowance is added. Score and slip.
  4. Stand the wall on top of the floor disk. Score, slip, compress, and bevel the contact edges as needed.
  5. Dry evenly. A test piece is the best way to tune shrinkage and fit.
