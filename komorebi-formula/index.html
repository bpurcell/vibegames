How the equations work

Each branch ends at x + L·cos(θ), y + L·sin(θ). Its children use your length and angle rules, then repeat up to the chosen depth.

r
Seeded random value, −1 to 1
d
Depth progress, 0 to 1
s
Branch side, −1 or +1 (trunk: 0)
a
Spread slider in radians
w
Wind slider, −1 to 1

Use + − * / ^, parentheses, pi, sin, cos, abs, sqrt, min, or max.

Try s*a*(1-d*0.5) + w*0.2 for branches that narrow toward the tips.

To keep growth bounded, length ratios are limited to 0.3–0.86, turns to ±1.6 radians, and curvature to ±0.9. Undefined results are rejected. Edits affect only the selected tree when you press Apply.
