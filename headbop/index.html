Head Bop! 🥁

A whole band in your body — drum with your head, make music with your hands!

Head = Drum

Stand back so the camera can see you, then bounce your head down like you're rocking out. Every bounce goes BOOM. 💥

Hands = Magic Notes

Raise a hand and it sings! 🎵 Higher hand → higher note, following the rainbow stripes. Use both hands for two notes at once. Every note always sounds good — promise. Drop your hands to make it quiet.

Bounce Mode

Tap the 🎵 button to switch how notes play:
Always On → hands sing the whole time
On Bounce → point with your hands, and the notes only pop when your head goes BOOM — drum and notes together, right on the beat!

Too easy? Too hard?

Use the picker in the corner:
🐢 Gentle → big bounces only
🙂 Normal → regular bounces
⚡ Bouncy → tiny bounces count

Keep the beat

Turn on the 🕰 metronome and try to boom right on the click!
