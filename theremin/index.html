Theremin Guide

A camera theremin: play notes and chords with your hands, no touching required. Based on Gesture Synth.

Right Hand — Pitch

Raise and lower your hand to pick the note. Higher hand → higher note, across three octaves (C3 to C6), snapped to standard tuning — every note lands in tune. The keyboard on the left edge shows the note you're on and lights up every note in the chord.

Left Hand — Chord & Volume

Fingers (Chord Type)
Fist / no hand → single note
1 → major
2 → minor
3 → dominant 7th
4 → diminished 7th

Height
Higher → louder, lower → softer. No left hand in view? Fixed volume.

Display

The big readout shows the note your right hand is on, with the chord type underneath.

Metronome

Use the 🕰 button (top right) to start a click track. Set the tempo with the BPM box (40–240). The first beat of each bar is accented.
